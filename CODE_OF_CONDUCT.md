# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Separate criticism of ideas from criticism of people.
- Handle biological, security, and dual-use concerns responsibly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other
contributions that violate these expectations. Report incidents privately to
the repository owner through GitHub. This policy is adapted from Contributor
Covenant 2.1.
